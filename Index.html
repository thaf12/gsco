About Us

GCSO is a premium coffee producer and exporter based in Ngozi, Burundi. We operate state-of-the-art washing stations and natural drying stations to deliver exceptional specialty coffee to international markets.

Our Processing

Our coffee is carefully hand-picked, processed through washed and natural methods, and meticulously dried to highlight the rich flavors of Burundi. Quality control is at the heart of everything we do.

Export

We export green specialty coffee to roasters around the globe. Our logistics team ensures safe and timely delivery, maintaining the high quality of our beans from farm to cup.
